## 1. WHO THIS IS FOR

Two audiences, in this order:

1. **A local business owner in Nashik** deciding whether to hire me. They are not designers. They will judge the site in 8 seconds on whether it feels expensive, modern and trustworthy, and whether it looks like something they would be proud to put on their own shop.
2. **A client who wants to build a website and wants to be shown what a good website actually is.**

My goal is to make that owner think: *"if he made this, he can make mine."* Not to impress other developers.

---

## 2. DESIGN RULES (non-negotiable)

**Do**
- Editorial layout. Asymmetric grid, deliberate off-centre balance, large type, generous whitespace.
- One type pairing: a high-contrast display serif for headlines (Instrument Serif or Fraunces) with a tight grotesque for everything else (Inter Tight). Headlines large enough that they are the actual visual, 64px+ on desktop.
- A fixed, restrained palette: bone `#F4F1EA`, ink `#111110`, terracotta `#C2542D` as the single accent, plus one cool accent (`#1A47FF`) used only for links and focus states.
- Real photography and real screenshots where they exist. Real proof is the design.
- Negative space as a material. Sections should breathe; do not fill every row.
- Motion that has a job: type that reveals on scroll, a plot/tile that responds to hover, page transitions under 400ms with a real ease curve. Nothing bouncy, nothing spinning.
- A dark mode toggle if it costs you nothing. Light mode is the primary design.

**Do not**
- No purple or indigo gradients. No glassmorphism. No glowing orbs. No "AI-generated landing page" look.
- No three-column grid of icon + title + two-line description as a way to present services.
- No stock photos of people in offices, no fake browser chrome screenshots, no invented URLs, no placeholder project names.
- No emoji as bullets. No lorem ipsum. No "Lorem-scale" filler numbers.
- Do not centre-align body text.
- Do not use words like "passionate", "innovative", "cutting-edge", "unleashing", "elevate", "journey". No exclamation marks.
- No dead links. If a URL does not exist, do not render a button that goes nowhere.

## 6. THE AI CHAT ASSISTANT ("Swaraj Copilot")

An embedded assistant that a visitor can talk to about me: my work, my skills, my services, my projects, how to get in touch, and roughly what a site from me involves.

Requirements:
- Answer from a single hard-coded knowledge object built out of the content in section 3. It must not invent projects, prices, dates or links that are not in that object.
- Be honest about pricing: I quote per project after understanding what is needed, so the assistant should say that and offer to pass the enquiry on. Never let it quote a rupee figure.
- If the visitor shows buying intent, it should push toward WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.
- Have a real fallback. If the model call fails, times out, or no key is present, the chat must degrade to a small keyword-matched responder over the same knowledge object so it never looks broken. Show a subtle "limited mode" note rather than an error.
- It is an assistant representing a real person: it should say so plainly if asked, and never pretend to be me.
- Design it to match the site: no default chat bubble UI, no purple gradient. It should look like a considered part of the page, and on mobile it should be a bottom sheet, not a full-screen takeover.
- Keep the system prompt short and strict: answer only from the knowledge object, keep replies under 70 words unless asked for detail, one question back at most.
